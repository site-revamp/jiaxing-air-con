# Jiaxing Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Jiaxing Aircon supplies, installs, services, and repairs air-conditioning systems for homes and businesses across Singapore. It also offers yearly maintenance contracts. The proposed homepage helps visitors choose a service, compare packages, and contact the company.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make key services easier to find.** The original homepage covers a broad range of services, making it harder to find the right starting point. A focused hero and six distinct service cards would bring installation, servicing, and maintenance into view, with a direct enquiry link on each card.
2. **Clarify package pricing and company credentials.** A consistent package layout would make prices easier to compare by showing what each package includes, any additional charges, and applicable warranty terms. A dedicated section would highlight the company’s stated operating history, team size, and service fleet alongside genuine reviews and verifiable certifications.
3. **Shorten the mobile enquiry path.** Customers seeking urgent repairs need quick access to contact options. A persistent mobile Call and WhatsApp bar, enquiry buttons beside services and packages, and a shorter contact form would make it easier to get in touch. Phone, email, and address details would be easy to locate.

## Design Improvements

Clear headings and consistent card layouts would make services and packages easier to scan. Placing package terms and enquiry buttons beside the relevant offer would keep the information needed to make a decision in one place.
